# The Intelligent Travel Program

Complimentary Sponsor Content Webinar and Summary Report

Business travel has resumed and most trips have multiple transportation moments: flights, ground transportation from the airport to the hotel, travel to client meetings, and more. Rather than treating travel and ground transportation as costs to manage, leading organizations are leveraging enterprise travel programs to increase financial discipline, strengthen operational resilience, and create better employee experiences. The benefits are wide ranging. Employees are more likely to conform to company policies, while spending less time on expense reports. Meanwhile, the finance and procurement teams have better visibility and control.
